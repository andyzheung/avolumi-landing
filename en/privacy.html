Privacy Policy

Avolumi is a baby log for parents and caregivers. It has no developer-operated server, account system, advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. The developer cannot access, view, or obtain the entries you make in the app.

Where your records live

The baby nickname, birth date, feeding, sleep, diaper, growth records, and app settings you enter are stored locally on your device. If your device is signed in to iCloud, Apple CloudKit can synchronise that data to your private iCloud database and your own devices.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, the app continues to use local storage and its core features remain available.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or health data.
  • We do not profile users or analyse behaviour, and we do not share, sell, or trade your data.
  • We do not require an Avolumi account, email address, or phone number.

Notifications, purchases, and exports

Avolumi uses local notifications for feeding intervals, timer completions, and weekly summaries; notifications are created and scheduled on your device. One-time in-app purchases, refunds, and Family Sharing are handled by the Apple App Store. We do not access or store payment information.

You can export CSV data, PDF summaries, and JSON backups. You decide where exported files are saved or shared, and you can delete local records in the app.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email andyzheung@aliyun.com. Please do not send children's personal information or complete records by email.
