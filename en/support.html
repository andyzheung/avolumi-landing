Common questions

How do I export my records?

In the app, go to Settings → Data to export CSV. Premium also supports PDF record summaries and JSON backups. You choose where each file is saved or shared.

Why is iCloud sync not working?

Check that every device is signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is enabled. Once your network is back, keep the app open briefly so the system can complete synchronisation. If the issue continues, include your device model, iOS version, and the time it happened in your email.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the Apple ID that made the purchase, then choose Restore Purchase in the upgrade page or Settings → Restore Purchase. Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store; you never need to provide payment details to us.
